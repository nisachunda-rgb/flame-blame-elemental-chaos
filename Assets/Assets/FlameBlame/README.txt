FLAME & BLAME - Elemental Chaos (Unity contest demo)

1. Drag this whole "FlameBlame" folder into your Unity project's Assets folder.
2. Press Play. The game builds itself in any scene (no setup).
3. Controls: WASD move, mouse aim, LMB/Space spray, E breaker, V view mode, R restart.

All tuning numbers are at the top of Scripts/GridSim.cs.
The level is the text map at the top of Scripts/FlameBlameGame.cs - edit the letters to change it.
